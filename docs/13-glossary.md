# Glossary of financial terms

These definitions are the inline tooltip explanations attached to linked terms throughout the pfg.bg financial-literacy articles (the `title=` hover text). Each term also has a full definition page at `https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/<id>`. 299 unique terms.

### "fast" loans _(also: "quick" loans)_
A short-term loan typically used to cover urgent financial needs and unexpected expenses.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/88

### "Green Card" certificate
A document certifying that the holder has a valid motor third-party liability insurance policy issued in their home country, covering the minimum mandatory liability limits required by law in the country being visited.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/393

### accident
A type of personal insurance that provides protection against risks to the life, health, and bodily integrity of the insured person in the event of an accident.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/557

### active
Income received in exchange for performed work or professional activity as a result of invested labour, time, and involvement.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/11

### Active investing
An approach where investors aim to outperform the return of a market index or other benchmark by taking advantage of market opportunities.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/66

### actual value
A basis for calculating compensation, where depreciation of the property is taken into account, so the amount paid is reduced by the corresponding depreciation percentage.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/128

### additional remuneration
Monetary payments in addition to the basic salary, paid to an employee depending on additional working conditions, achieved results, or the specific nature of the work performed.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/34

### additional voluntary pension insurance
This represents the third pillar of the pension system in Bulgaria.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/146

### advance fee fraud
A type of financial fraud in which the perpetrator convinces the victim to pay an upfront fee (advance) with the promise of receiving a substantial loan, inheritance, investment opportunity, reward, participation in a deal, or other financial benefit.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/208

### advance tax
A tax that is paid in advance in instalments on estimated or already received income
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/1

### Annual Percentage Rate of Charge (APR)
An indicator expressing the total cost of a loan as an annual percentage.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/112

### annuities
Regular payments for a fixed term or for life, usually as part of pension or life insurance products, intended to provide a steady income.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/380

### asset _(also: assets)_
Any resource that has value and can be owned, controlled, or used by an individual or legal entity with the aim of generating future economic benefits.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/64

### ATM
An automated electronic device that allows bank customers to perform basic banking transactions without the assistance of a bank employee.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/78

### bank _(also: banks)_
A financial institution that offers services such as storing money, providing loans, making payments, exchanging currency, and managing savings and investments.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/14

### banking products
A service or instrument provided by a commercial bank intended to meet the financial needs of individuals and legal entities.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/76

### Barter
Exchanging goods or services without using money.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/47

### basic payment account
A type of bank product primarily intended for payments.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/348

### basic salary
Payment from the employer to the employee for work performed.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/39

### bequest
A testamentary disposition that relates to a particular asset or property.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/169

### blockchain technology
A method of storing information within a computer network, consisting of a continuously growing list of records, called “blocks”, which are linked together and encrypted cryptographically.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/85

### bond _(also: bonds)_
A debt security through which the issuer (a government, municipality, company, or financial institution) undertakes to pay the bondholder a specified amount of money (the principal) on a predetermined date (maturity), as well as agreed interest payments (coupon payments) over the life of the bond.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/306

### borrowed
A financial instrument in which a bank or another financial institution (the lender) provides monetary funds to an individual or legal entity (the borrower) under the obligation to repay them within an agreed period and under specific conditions.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/17

### borrower _(also: borrowers)_
An individual or legal entity that receives money, goods, or services on loan from a private individual or organization, not necessarily through a licensed financial institution (lender), with an obligation to return them within a specified period and under agreed conditions, usually in exchange for interest.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/176

### brokers
A natural or legal person acting as an intermediary between investors and financial markets by executing orders for the purchase or sale of securities on behalf and for the account of clients, receiving remuneration (commission) for brokerage services.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/87

### budget
A money management plan showing expected income and planned expenses for a specific period of time (e.g. a month or a year) or a particular event.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/89

### Casco
Voluntary property insurance that covers damage to the policyholder’s own vehicle caused by various events.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/230

### cash transfer
A payment in which the payer provides cash without having a payment account in their name, with the purpose of transferring the funds to another person's account.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/277

### civil contracts
A contract concluded between two or more natural or legal persons regulating their rights and obligations regarding a specific transaction or service.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/113

### claim _(also: claim for compensation)_
An official request submitted to the insurer by the policyholder, beneficiary, or a third party who has suffered a loss, for the payment of an insurance sum or compensation upon the occurrence of an insured event covered by the policy.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/365

### claim _(also: claims, damage)_
Any reduction in property, loss of profit, or non-material consequences (such as pain and suffering) resulting from a specific event, typically related to unlawful conduct, omission, or an incident, which is subject to compensation under the law. Damage encompasses both material and non-material harm.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/443

### Collectible items
Items such as paintings, coins, or antiques that that are of interest to investors primarily because of the expectation that their value will increase over time.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/234

### commissions
A fee paid to a person or organisation for mediation, rendering a service, or facilitating a transaction.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/235

### commodity loan _(also: Commodity loans)_
A type of consumer credit granted for the purchase of a specific item on instalment, provided at a specific retail outlet.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/411

### compensation _(also: insurance compensation)_
Insurance compensation (insurance indemnity) — the monetary amount that the insurer pays to the insured, a third-party beneficiary, or the harmed party upon the occurrence of an insured event, with the purpose of compensating for the incurred losses within the agreed insurance coverage.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/188

### complaint
A written or oral statement by a natural or legal person expressing dissatisfaction or objection to the actions, decisions, or services of an institution, authority, enterprise, or individual, requesting an investigation and the taking of measures to resolve the problem or restore infringed rights.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/164

### Compound interest
Interest calculated by adding the interest from the previous period to the principal, so that the interest in the next period is calculated on the increased total.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/398

### conciliation commissions
An authority under the Commission for Consumer Protection (CCP), established for out-of-court resolution of disputes between consumers and suppliers of products and services.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/353

### consumer _(also: Consumer loans)_
A loan for individuals intended to finance various current needs, such as home repairs, purchases of goods and services, travel, education, or other expenses.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/358

### Consumer protection
A set of rights, measures, and mechanisms provided by law aimed at guaranteeing the safety, economic interests, right to information, and fair treatment of individuals when purchasing goods and services.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/201

### contract for difference (CFD) _(also: Contracts for Difference (CFDs))_
A contract in which the buyer does not actually own the underlying asset (stocks, commodities, gold, currencies, crypto assets, etc.) but agrees with the seller of the contract for difference to settle the difference in price between the present and a future point in time.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/144

### Contributory service
The period during which a person has been mandatorily or voluntarily insured, and for which social security contributions have been paid or are due in accordance with the applicable legislation.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/323

### coverage _(also: Insurance Coverage)_
The scope of rights and obligations arising from the insurance contract, under which the insurer undertakes to pay compensation or the sum insured upon the occurrence of certain events (risks), within the agreed terms, limits, periods, and exclusions.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/189

### credit agreement
An agreement under which the bank undertakes to provide the borrower with a specified amount of money for a defined purpose and under agreed terms and duration.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/143

### credit card
A payment card that represents a type of revolving credit.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/244

### credit history
Information about an individual’s past and current loans, including their amount and number, repayment behaviour, whether the individual has credit cards and how much they owe relative to their credit limits, as well as details about owned movable and immovable property.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/243

### credit intermediary
A natural or legal person who, in return for remuneration, provides intermediary services in the offering or conclusion of credit agreements with consumers, as well as advice and consultations related to such agreements.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/240

### Credit risk
The risk that a borrower or counterparty will fail to meet their financial obligations, such as repaying a credit or paying interest on time.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/242

### credit score
A numerical assessment of an individual’s creditworthiness, indicating how reliable they are in repaying loans.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/241

### creditor
A financial instrument in which a bank or another financial institution (the lender) provides monetary funds to an individual or legal entity (the borrower) under the obligation to repay them within an agreed period and under specific conditions.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/239

### creditor _(also: creditors)_
An individual or legal entity, including a bank or licensed financial institution, that is legally authorised to provide loans or credits and has the right to demand repayment of financial obligations from the borrower or loan recipient.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/247

### creditworthiness
A comprehensive indicator reflecting the borrower’s ability to meet their financial obligations to creditors on time and in full.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/248

### crowdfunding platforms _(also: Peer-to-peer lending and crowdfunding platforms)_
An online crowdfunding platform that connects investors with enterprises or individuals seeking financing for projects.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/349

### crypto asset _(also: Crypto assets, crypto-assets)_
A digital asset that uses cryptography and blockchain technology for security, ownership verification, and transaction execution.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/249

### cryptocurrencies
A crypto-asset and means of payment based on blockchain or similar technology and cryptography, which allows transactions to be carried out without the involvement of traditional financial institutions.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/250

### current account
A bank product designed for the daily management of funds, including deposits, withdrawals, and making payments. A debit card is often issued with the account.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/372

### customs duties
A tax imposed on the import or export of goods when crossing the borders of a country or a customs union, aimed at protecting the domestic market, promoting local production, and generating revenue for the state budget.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/270

### CVV/CVC
The last three digits (for VISA and MasterCard) located on the back of the payment card, to the right, in the white signature strip.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/6

### cybersecurity
A set of policies, technologies, processes, and actions aimed at protecting computer systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access, malicious attacks, damage, information leakage, or service disruption.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/232

### dealers
In securities trading, an individual or legal entity that buys and sells securities on its own behalf and account with the aim of making a profit from the price difference (buy and sell prices).
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/135

### debit card
A payment instrument in the form of a plastic card with a magnetic stripe and/or chip containing information about the linked payment account.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/126

### debt
An obligation of one party (the debtor) to repay another party (the creditor) a specified amount of money under agreed conditions such as term, interest, repayment method, etc.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/154

### debt-to-income ratio _(also: debt-to-income ratio (DTI))_
A term denoting the debt-to-income ratio (DTI), which indicates the percentage of a consumer's monthly gross income allocated to debt repayment.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/233

### deductible _(also: deductibles)_
In insurance, it is the insured’s percentage or fixed contribution to the total amount of a claim, with the remaining amount covered by the insurer.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/388

### deposit _(also: deposits)_
Funds deposited by individuals or legal entities in accounts with credit institutions, guaranteed by law up to a certain limit.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/103

### derivative product
A financial instrument whose value depends on or is linked to the price of another asset called the underlying asset.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/130

### digital national currencies
A digital form of a country’s national currency, issued and fully backed by the central bank. CBDCs represent a new type of money that exists only in electronic form, intended to provide a secure and efficient means of payment and store of value.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/442

### direct _(also: direct taxes)_
A tax paid by the end consumer but collected and remitted by another party (usually the seller or supplier) when goods or services are sold.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/371

### disability pensions due to general illness
Granted to individuals with a permanent reduction in work capacity of 50% or more.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/338

### disposable income
The income that households or individuals have available for spending and saving after deducting all taxes and compulsory social contributions.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/373

### diversification
An investment risk management strategy where funds are distributed across different assets, sectors, markets, or financial instruments to reduce the impact of potential losses from any single investment on the overall portfolio.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/132

### dividend _(also: dividends)_
The distribution of a portion of a company’s profit, paid as a reward to its owners—partners or shareholders.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/133

### donation tax
A local direct tax payable on the gratuitous transfer of property (money, goods, real estate, etc.) between individuals, where such transfer does not constitute inheritance.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/116

### down payment
The amount of money you have available and invest when purchasing an asset with a loan — for example, a home or a car.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/389

### Early repayment
Full or partial repayment of a loan obligation before the contractually agreed term.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/364

### electronic money companies
A type of financial institution authorised to issue electronic money and provide payment services.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/151

### Emergency Fund _(also: "emergency" fund)_
A type of safety net designed to cover unforeseen expenses.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/436

### employment contract
A written agreement between an employee and an employer, specifying the employee’s duties, working hours, remuneration, and other rights and obligations under the Labour Code. I
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/33

### endowment life insurance (classic)
An insurance product that combines life and health risk protection with a savings component.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/403

### event _(also: insured, insured event)_
A future, uncertain event, beyond the control of the parties, the occurrence of which gives rise to the insurer’s obligation to provide the agreed insurance coverage, usually in the form of payment of compensation or the sum insured.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/191

### Exchange-traded funds (ETFs _(also: Exchange-traded funds (ETFs))_
A collective investment scheme that tracks the performance of a specific market index, commodity, currency, or other financial asset, and is traded on the stock exchange like shares.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/86

### Excise duties
An indirect tax that is levied and paid upon the production, import, or supply within the territory of the country of excise goods such as alcoholic beverages, tobacco products, energy products and fuels, electricity, and passenger cars (in certain cases).
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/68

### expenses
An amount of money spent on purchasing goods, services, or carrying out a specific activity.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/23

### fee _(also: Fees)_
A monetary payment required for the provision of a specific service by a government body, municipality, institution, or private organization.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/415

### financial goals
A value-based measure of a planned personal objective, specifying the funds required to achieve it and the time needed to secure them.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/43

### financial instrument _(also: financial institutions, financial instruments)_
Contractual rights or obligations with monetary value that can be traded or invested.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/553

### financial leases _(also: financial leasing)_
A contract in which the lessor grants the lessee the right to use a long-term asset (e.g. car, machine, equipment) in exchange for periodic payments with an option or obligation to acquire ownership of the asset at the end of the contract.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/428

### financial plan _(also: financial plans)_
A structured strategic document outlining how an individual, household, or organization will manage income, expenses, savings, investments, and liabilities to achieve specific financial goals, such as retirement, home purchase, education, business development, or risk protection.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/429

### financial sustainability
The ability of an individual or organization to successfully carry out its functions in a changing financial environment.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/432

### Fines
A type of administrative penalty, which is a monetary sanction imposed on a natural or legal person for committing an administrative violation specified in law or subordinate legislation.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/111

### fiscal decentralisation
The process by which certain powers to collect and allocate public funds are delegated by the central government to local self-government authorities.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/434

### fixed interest rate
A constant interest rate applied for the entire term of the contract or for a specified period thereof.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/426

### Fixed surcharge
The margin representing the credit risk borne by the bank when issuing a loan to an individual borrower.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/427

### floating interest rate _(also: variable interest rate)_
According to Bulgarian legislation, a floating interest rate is composed of two components: a reference interest rate (RIR) and a fixed margin.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/343

### Freelancers
An individual who practices a liberal profession and carries out professional activity on their own account, is not registered as a sole trader, and is self-insured.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/262

### fundamental analysis
A primary tool for assessing the potential of a given asset, which involves examining financial statements, forecasting revenue and expense growth, and analysing industry trends and economic conditions.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/437

### general insurance
Includes insurance policies that cover property, liability, and other non-life risks (for example, motor comprehensive insurance, property insurance, health insurance, accident insurance, etc.).
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/313

### gold _(also: Investment gold)_
Physical gold of high purity, purchased mainly as a store of value and as protection against inflation and market risks, rather than as a raw material for jewellery or industry.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/218

### gold standard
A monetary system in which the unit of currency is directly linked to a fixed quantity of gold, and banknotes could be exchanged for that amount.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/204

### Government securities
Debt financial instruments issued by a national government, through its executive authority, to raise funds for public spending, refinance existing debt, or cover temporary budget deficits.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/160

### grace period
In the case of credit cards, this is a period of time, usually between 45 and 60 days, during which no interest is charged on the outstanding balance if it is paid in full by the specified due date.  For loans, it is a period during which the borrower may benefit from reduced monthly instalments.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/114

### gross salary
The total amount of money accrued to an employee by the employer before deductions for taxes and other contributions.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/31

### guarantor
A third party involved in a contract with a credit institution, who guarantees with all their assets the repayment of the debt if the borrower fails to fulfil their obligations.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/356

### guarantor for a loan
A person designated to repay the borrowed debt if the borrower is unable to do so.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/108

### health insurance
The activity of collecting health insurance contributions and premiums, managing the collected funds, and using them to finance healthcare services, activities, and goods provided for under the Health Insurance Act, national framework agreements, and insurance contracts.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/202

### health insurance
The activity of collecting health insurance contributions and premiums, managing the collected funds, and using them to finance healthcare services, activities, and goods provided for under the Health Insurance Act, national framework agreements, and insurance contracts.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/54

### health insurance contribution _(also: health insurance contributions)_
An amount payable for compulsory health insurance, calculated as a percentage of the insurable income determined under the Health Insurance Act.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/203

### high-yield investment programs (HYIP)
A type of Ponzi scheme, an investment fraud that promises unsustainably high returns by paying earlier investors with funds collected from new investors.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/97

### highly liquid assets
An asset that can be quickly and easily converted into cash with minimal loss of value.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/98

### home insurance _(also: Property Insurance)_
A type of insurance under which the insurer undertakes to compensate the insured for property damage suffered as a result of an insured event specified in the insurance contract.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/209

### identity theft
The unlawful acquisition and misuse of personal data and financial information—such as names, national identification numbers, bank account details, or credit card information—without the consent of the rightful owner.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/238

### illiquid assets
An asset that is difficult to sell or convert into cash quickly without a significant loss of value or a considerable delay.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/294

### immovable property tax _(also: tax on property acquisition for consideration)_
A local direct tax paid annually by owners (or users in certain cases) of buildings, apartments, houses, plots, and other real estate located within the territory of Bulgaria.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/120

### in kind _(also: Collateral)_
A security provided by the borrower or a third party in the form of a tangible asset that has value and can be used by the lender to recover the debt in case of default.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/93

### Income
Monetary funds or monetary equivalents received within a certain period from sources such as salary, self-employment, property, pensions, social benefits and allowances, regular transfers from other households, as well as one-off income from sales, etc., including valued in-kind income.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/29

### Indebtedness
One of the key criteria in assessing creditworthiness, which involves analysing a person’s current obligations — loans, leases, and others.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/172

### indemnities _(also: compensation)_
Insurance compensation (insurance indemnity) — the monetary amount that the insurer pays to the insured, a third-party beneficiary, or the harmed party upon the occurrence of an insured event, with the purpose of compensating for the incurred losses within the agreed insurance coverage.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/302

### index mutual funds
Collective investment schemes designed to track and replicate the performance of a specific market index, such as SOFIX, S&P 500, or others.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/222

### indices
A market indicator that measures the average performance of a specific group of assets—usually stocks, bonds, or other financial instruments—grouped by a certain characteristic (e.g. sector, region, market capitalization).
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/221

### indirect _(also: indirect tax, Indirect taxes)_
A tax paid by the end consumer but collected and remitted by another party (usually the seller or supplier) when goods or services are sold.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/237

### individual account
A personal account of the insured person in a pension fund, where all contributions, the returns on their investment, deductions for fees, and other transactions are recorded in accordance with the fund’s rules.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/224

### Individual coefficient
One of the four components used in the formula for calculating pensions.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/223

### inflation
A general increase in the overall level of prices of goods and services in the economy, leading to a decrease in the purchasing power of money.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/226

### inheritance
The totality of property rights and obligations that pass from a deceased person (decedent/ testator) to one or more living persons (heirs) by law or by will.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/281

### inheritance tax
A local direct tax imposed on property inherited by law or by will (movable and immovable assets, property rights, receivables, and liabilities) located in Bulgaria or abroad by Bulgarian citizens, as well as on property in Bulgaria inherited by foreign citizens.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/119

### insurable income
The income on which social security contributions are calculated and paid in accordance with the law.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/36

### Insurance _(also: insurance products)_
The assumption of risks in exchange for receiving a premium, whereby the insurer undertakes to provide protection to the insured upon the occurrence of certain events (risks) agreed in the insurance contract.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/177

### insurance
A product offered by an insurer that provides protection against specified risks.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/192

### Insurance agents
A natural person or a trader, registered and licensed under the applicable legislation, authorized to act on behalf of one or more insurers, to offer insurance products, provide information about coverage, and assist clients in concluding insurance contracts.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/180

### Insurance brokers
A company or a sole proprietor, registered and licensed in accordance with applicable regulations, who carries out insurance intermediation on behalf of and for the account of the policyholder, insured, or beneficiary.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/181

### insurance company _(also: insurance companies, insurance company (insurer))_
A legal entity licensed by the Financial Supervision Commission, which carries out insurance activities in accordance with the applicable legislation.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/187

### insurance contract
An agreement concluded between an insurer and a policyholder when purchasing an insurance policy, under which the insurer undertakes to provide protection against specified risks, and the insured or policyholder pays the agreed premium.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/182

### insurance intermediation
Activities involving the provision of services related to the offering, consulting, preparation, and conclusion of insurance contracts, as well as assisting in their management and execution, including upon the occurrence of an insured event.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/190

### insured
A person whose life, health, property, or liability is the subject of insurance coverage under an insurance contract.
Definition page: https://pfg.bg/en/glossary/178
